# Sitefile Pilot Pack

Working materials for the week-of-28-Jul recruitment step in
[GO-LIVE-PLAN.md](./GO-LIVE-PLAN.md). Three parts: who to pick, what to
say, and the one-pager the contractor keeps.

---

## 1 · Picking the pilot (checklist)

Score each candidate — the winner needs all three from the plan, the
rest are tiebreakers:

**Must have (from the plan):**
- [ ] Currently produces weekly/monthly progress reports **manually**
      (Word/photos-in-email counts; if they already use a tool, skip)
- [ ] Site team uses smartphones and takes photos anyway
- [ ] Relationship warm enough to tolerate rough edges and give honest feedback

**Tiebreakers:**
- [ ] Has a programme in MS Project / Excel they can hand over (import works
      for XML, Excel and now PDF)
- [ ] Their client actually reads progress reports (success criterion #2
      requires the client to open one)
- [ ] Job runs through August–September without a natural break
- [ ] One named person owns reporting today (that's your champion and
      your feedback channel)

**Disqualifiers:** job ends before a full reporting cycle; reporting is
done by head office rather than site; any T&T optics risk you can't
answer with the prepared line.

## 2 · The 15-minute pitch (script skeleton)

Framing rules from the plan: free pilot, feedback-for-value trade, no
mention of consultancy plans, pricing tiers, or anything that signals
departure. If asked "whose tool is this?": *"Something I built myself,
outside work — it's disclosed, and you'd be using it free as a pilot."*

**Open (2 min)** — their pain, not the product:
> "How long does your monthly progress report take you — chasing photos
> off the lads' phones, matching them to the programme, formatting the
> document?" *(Let them answer. Typical: hours-to-a-day per cycle.)*

**Show (8 min)** — the loop, on your phone + laptop:
1. Phone: take a photo on the capture screen — point out GPS and time
   are recorded automatically.
2. Laptop: photo appears in the project; link it to a programme task in
   two clicks (show the AI suggestion with its plain-English reason).
3. Generate the report: show the finished PDF — **their logo, their
   colour**, narrative summary, programme chart, photo records,
   verification page. "This took one click."

**Offer (3 min)** — the trade:
> "I want one contractor to run this on a real job for a reporting
> cycle. Free — not a trial, no card, nothing. You get your reporting
> time back; I get told everything that's wrong with it. If after a
> cycle you'd genuinely pay £99 a month for it, we talk. If not, you've
> lost nothing."

**Close (2 min)** — the ask, small and concrete:
> "Give me the programme for the job and 15 minutes with whoever runs
> your reporting. I'll have it set up the same day."

### Sitefile — getting started (2 minutes)

**1 · Sign up** — on your laptop: **www.sitefile.app** → Get started →
use your work email. You'll get a code by email — check spam the first
time.

**2 · Your project is set up for you.** Your programme tasks are already
loaded. Your logo and your client's logo are on the reports.

**On site (phone):**
- Open **www.sitefile.app** on your phone → Capture
- Take photos as you normally would — location and time are recorded
  automatically
- Add a one-line note when it's useful ("pit 1 excavated, awaiting
  inspection") — your notes appear in the client report

**In the office (laptop):**
- **Evidence** tab: check photos landed, accept the suggested task links
  (or pick from the list)
- **Reports** tab → Generate Report → pick the period → sign → done.
  Send the PDF to your client as usual — it's password-protected if you
  set a password.

**Rules of thumb:** photos any day, links once a week, report once a
cycle. Nothing to install — it's a website that works on any phone.

**Stuck?** WhatsApp Derian: **[PHONE]** — replies same evening.
Anything confusing or broken, say so bluntly: that's the deal.
